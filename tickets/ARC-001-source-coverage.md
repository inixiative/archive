# ARC-001: Understand where the useful experience lives

**Status:** FF — Future feature; outside MVP

## Why this matters

The initial deployment must capture the work that contains real decisions and human corrections. Locally available histories may represent only part of that work.

## Goal

Identify the relevant Claude Code, Codex, and hosted-session workflows used at UserEvidence, and establish what experience Archive can obtain from each.

## Desired outcomes

- The team knows which actual workflows the initial release covers and which it does not.
- Coverage includes consideration of hosted sessions, rather than assuming all useful history is on a local machine.
- Missing source content and its effect on retrospective usefulness are explicit.

## Questions to resolve

Which sources contain most of the corrections we want to learn from, and which are necessary for the first useful deployment?

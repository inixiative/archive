# ARC-008: Demonstrate that captured experience supports learning

**Status:** FF — Future feature; outside MVP

## Why this matters

More stored sessions do not establish that an agent team is becoming more competent. Archive needs a concrete demonstration that its evidence is useful.

## Goal

Use real UserEvidence work for an initial retrospective that recovers human corrections, connects them to available outcomes, and proposes contextual lessons with source references.

## Desired outcomes

- A retrospective consumer can find and cite actual corrections from captured sessions.
- The proposed lessons retain the context needed to judge where they apply.
- Derived interpretations remain distinguishable from the original evidence.
- The exercise reveals whether important evidence is missing or difficult to retrieve.
- The team can identify a recurring correction to watch for in subsequent work; the retrospective alone is not treated as proof of improvement.

## Questions to resolve

Which recurring class of correction would make the first demonstration meaningful to the people supervising agents?

# ARC-004: Carry selected experience across machines and teammates

**Status:** Proposed goal

## Why this matters

Experience confined to one machine cannot reliably inform work on another machine or help another team member.

## Goal

Make selected local experience available in the shared work Archive when connected, while allowing local work to continue offline.

## Desired outcomes

- Selected experience becomes available to authorized teammates and agents.
- Offline periods, interrupted transfers, and restarts do not cause silent loss or duplicate results.
- Users can distinguish experience that is local, shared, pending, or unable to sync.
- The first work deployment can be useful with one shared destination.

## Questions to resolve

What should users expect when an already shared session changes, is removed, or becomes unavailable at its source?
